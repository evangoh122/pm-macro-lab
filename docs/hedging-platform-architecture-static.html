Prediction Market Hedging Platform Architecture

Three data providers flow through a Databricks Delta lakehouse and a backtesting engine to a research dashboard.

Data sources

  • • Polygon / Massive: TLT bars, options, volatility
  • • IBKR ForecastTrader: event-contract quotes
  • • FRED / ALFRED: macro series and vintages
  • • Federal Reserve: FOMC calendar and outcomes

Lakehouse layers

  • • Bronze: raw responses with timestamps
  • • Silver: normalized prices, quotes, events
  • • Gold: point-in-time research datasets
  • • Signal time kept apart from execution time

Strategies compared

  • • Long TLT, unhedged
  • • Long TLT + protective put or put spread
  • • Long TLT + IBKR YES contract on a Fed hike
  • • Long TLT + options and event contracts combined
